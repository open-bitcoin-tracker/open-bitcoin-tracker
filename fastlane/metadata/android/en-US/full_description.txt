Open Bitcoin Tracker is a free, open-source Android app for tracking the value of your bitcoin. Enter holdings manually; OBT does not hold funds, manage keys, or send transactions.

Features:
- Live BTC prices and chart history from Kraken.
- Named stacks with custom colors or images and a combined portfolio total.
- Historical value charts with bundled data for offline viewing and future-value projections.
- A converter for BTC, satoshis, and seven fiat currencies.
- PIN or biometric stack lock, with encryption when lock is enabled.
- A full-screen price view, four theme palettes, and ten languages.

Holdings, settings, and stack images stay on your device. No accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or telemetry.
